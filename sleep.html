Sleep

Prioritizing sleep helps avoid feeling overwhelmed, being low in energy, and keeps you feeling your self. There is a strong relationship between sleep and mental health that should be tailored towards. How much sleep you get daily affects how your body functions, so another way to a perfect day, is to get some sleep!

Try these strategies tonight!

  1. 1. Go to bed and wake up at similar times each day
  2. 2. Limit screen time before bed
  3. 3. Create a calm, comfortable place to sleep
